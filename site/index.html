Turn the invoice PDF you already have into an e-invoice.

Your layout stays exactly as it is. The invoice data is added as XML, following EN 16931 (ZUGFeRD / Factur-X).

Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser.

Read with AI

A language model reads names, addresses and line items from the text of your invoice. It runs on your device; your invoice does not leave the browser.

The model is downloaded from huggingface.co on first use and kept in the browser.

The text of your invoice is sent to this address. If it is not on your device, the text leaves the browser.

    What is added to your file

    The page stays as it is. Behind it, the same invoice is written down a second time, so that software can read it.

    Printed on your invoice Written into the XML
    Rechnung Nr. RE-2026-0042 <ram:ID>RE-2026-0042</ram:ID>
    Rechnungsdatum 03.11.2026 <udt:DateTimeString format="102">20261103</udt:DateTimeString>
    Muster Software GmbH <ram:SellerTradeParty><ram:Name>Muster Software GmbH</ram:Name>
    Beratung, 10 Std. zu 100,00 € <ram:BilledQuantity unitCode="HUR">10</ram:BilledQuantity>
    Umsatzsteuer 19 % 284,81 € <ram:CalculatedAmount>284.81</ram:CalculatedAmount>
    Gesamtbetrag 1.783,81 € <ram:GrandTotalAmount>1783.81</ram:GrandTotalAmount>

    People read the left side; the software of your customer and of the tax office reads the right one. Both have to say the same, which is why every value is compared with your PDF before the file is created.

    What you should know

    • Only the issuer can issue an e-invoice. A PDF you received does not become a valid e-invoice by converting it yourself.
    • In an e-invoice the XML counts, not the picture. You are responsible for the data being right.
    • A strict PDF/A-3 requires your PDF to embed its fonts. If they are missing, the page tells you.
    • Discounts on the whole invoice have no field of their own yet: enter them as a line with a negative quantity.
    • This page is a tool, not tax advice.

    Privacy

    There is no server that sees your invoice, no cookies and no tracking. Only if you choose “Read with AI” does the browser download a model from huggingface.co, once; your invoice is not transmitted.

    For developers and AI assistants

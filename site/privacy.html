Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit this website, why, and what rights you have. The pdf-to-einvoice library, command line tool and MCP server run on your own machine; this policy covers only the website.

  • No cookies, accounts, forms, analytics or advertising.
  • Invoices you open are processed in your browser. They are never uploaded.
  • GitHub delivers the site and processes connection data to do so.
  • If you choose “Read with AI”, your browser downloads a language model from Hugging Face and GitHub. Your invoice is not sent there.

Your invoices

The PDF you choose or drop is read, checked and turned into an e-invoice on your device. It is never uploaded, and we do not receive it, its name or anything in it. The same goes for the data you type into the form and for the file you download.

Reading with AI (optional)

If you choose “Read with AI” with a model that runs in your browser, the browser downloads the model once: its files from huggingface.co (Hugging Face, Inc., 20 Jay Street, Suite 620, Brooklyn, NY 11201, USA) and its runtime from raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub, Inc.). Both receive your IP address and the technical data of the request, as with any download. Neither receives your invoice: the model reads it on your device.

The download starts only when you start it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in offering the function you asked for (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you do not use it, no request is made.

If you enter the address of your own model service instead, the text of your invoice is sent to that address. That is your choice and under your control; the page says so next to the field.

Data stored in your browser

The site remembers your language in your browser's local storage under lang. A model you downloaded is kept in the browser's cache so that it need not be downloaded again. Both stay on your device and are never sent to us. They are strictly necessary for functions you use (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is needed; you can delete them anytime in your browser settings.

Your invoices

The PDF you choose or drop is read, checked and turned into an e-invoice on your device. It is never uploaded, and we do not receive it, its name or anything in it. The same goes for the data you type into the form and for the file you download.

Reading with AI (optional)

If you choose “Read with AI” with a model that runs in your browser, the browser downloads the model once: its files from huggingface.co and its download servers under hf.co (Hugging Face, Inc., 20 Jay Street, Suite 620, Brooklyn, NY 11201, USA) and its program code from raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub, Inc.). Both receive your IP address and the technical data of the request, as with any download. Neither receives your invoice: the model reads it on your device.

The download starts only when you start it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in offering the function you asked for (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you do not use it, no request is made.

If you enter the address of your own model service instead, the text of your invoice is sent to that address. That is your choice and under your control; the page says so next to the field.

Analytics with Umami

We count page views with Umami, open-source analytics that we run ourselves at analytics.smart-byte.com on a server of Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, located in Finland. Hetzner processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR); nobody else receives it. When a page loads, and when you create a file, a small script sends:

  • the page's address, without query string or fragment, and its title;
  • the website you came from, which browsers usually share only as a domain;
  • that a file was created and whether it is an e-invoice or an XRechnung, but never anything of your invoice, its name or its content;
  • your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, and language;
  • your approximate location (country, region, and city), looked up from your IP address on our server.

Umami sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It does not store your IP address: to tell visits apart, it derives a pseudonymous identifier from your IP address, your browser's user agent, and a salt that changes every day, so visits cannot be linked across days. We look at the results only in aggregate, to see whether and how the site is used. We don't build profiles or combine this data with anything else.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: analytics records are deleted after 13 months.
  • Your choice: Umami doesn't count browsers that send Do Not Track, and you can turn it off for this browser here.

Data stored in your browser

The site remembers your language and, once you have switched it, the light or dark theme in your browser's local storage under lang and theme. A model you downloaded is kept in the browser's cache so that it need not be downloaded again. Both stay on your device and are never sent to us. They are strictly necessary for functions you use (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is needed; you can delete them at any time in your browser settings.

If you turn the counting of your visits off, the site notes that in the same place under umami.disabled.
